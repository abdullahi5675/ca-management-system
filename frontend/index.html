Powerful System Features

Built for Precision & Transparency

Designed to streamline continuous assessment scores submission, validation, and dispute management for tertiary institutions.

Flexible CA Allocation

Lecturers customize max scores per component (Assignments, Quizzes, Attendance, Tests, Presentations/Others) summing up to exactly 40 marks.

Multi-Department Courses

Assign one course across multiple departments (e.g. Computer Science, Software Engineering, EEE) without duplicating course entries.

Smart Student Auto-Link

Scores entered for unregistered students are securely stored as draft state and auto-match immediately when the student registers on the portal.

Official Result Score Sheet

Students view published CA scores rendered as formal university result slips, complete with total percentages and 1-click PDF export.

Grade Dispute Resolution

Students file complaints directly against specific course components. Lecturers review, respond, and resolve disputes transparently.

Bulk CSV Upload & Autocomplete

Upload entire class mark sheets via CSV or enter manually using smart registration number auto-completion.

How the Portal Operates

01

Course Catalog Setup

Faculty members create session courses, define 40-mark component weight limits, and assign participating departments.

02

Score Entry & Draft Saving

Scores are entered manually or uploaded in bulk. Non-registered student matric numbers are saved as draft records.

03

Publishing & Verification

Lecturers verify total distributions and publish grades. Registered students instantly see official score sheets.

04

Dispute & Resolution Tracker

Students flag errors with reason details. Lecturers review and update scores if discrepancies are confirmed.
